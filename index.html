01 · A letter for you

To the girl I keep choosing

My love,

Happy birthday to the person who has become such a beautiful part of my life. I wish I could be beside you today, steal a proper hug, look at you while you smile, and tell you all of this without a screen between us.

Sometimes I still think about how strange and beautiful it is that two people can be so far apart and yet somehow feel so close. Out of all the people in this enormous world, I found you. And somehow, talking to you can make an ordinary day feel like something I want to remember.

I don't love you only for the big moments. I love the little things too — your voice, your songs, the way you talk about the things you care about, the silly conversations, the quiet moments, and all the tiny pieces of you that I have slowly come to know.

And when I imagine my future, I don't imagine some perfect movie version of life. I imagine real days: laughing over stupid things, having difficult days and choosing patience, celebrating little victories, making memories, travelling, growing, and simply being there for each other. When I picture those years, I keep finding you in them.

I can't promise that life will always be easy. But I can tell you what my heart wants: you are the person I want to keep choosing. I want to keep learning you, loving you, supporting you, and building something that lasts. If life gives me the choice again and again, I want my answer to keep being you.

So today, on your birthday, I hope you know this: you are loved. Deeply, honestly, and in all the quiet ways that don't always fit into words.

Feliz cumpleaños, mi amor. May this year be gentle with your heart, full of beautiful surprises, and full of reasons to smile.

Te amo muchísimo. ♡
